# Blimp: Google Play store listing

## App name (30 characters max)

Blimp: Offline Music Player

## Short description (80 characters max)

Beautiful offline music player with a live visualizer, lyrics and hi-res audio.

## Full description (4000 characters max)

Blimp plays the music already on your phone, and makes it look and sound great.
No accounts, no ads, no internet. Your music and your listening stay on your device.

LIVE VISUALIZER
Watch your music move with a real-time visualizer that reacts to every beat. Choose from
five styles: Bars, Mirror, Wave, Radial and Pulse, drawn at your screen's full refresh
rate, up to 120Hz.

LYRICS
Read along with synced lyrics that light up line by line, and tap any line to jump to it.
Blimp reads lyrics embedded in your files or from .lrc files stored next to them.

SOUNDS RIGHT
• Hi-res badges show which of your songs are better than CD quality
• Volume normalisation plays every song at about the same loudness
• Smooth fades between songs
• Built-in equalizer
• Skip silence, and playback speed from 0.5× to 2×

YOUR LIBRARY, YOUR WAY
• Browse by songs, albums, artists, playlists or genre, or switch to your phone's folders
• Filter albums into Albums, EPs, Singles and Compilations
• Songs with several artists show up under each of them
• Multi-disc albums play in the right order
• Search that ignores accents and punctuation, so "beyonce" finds Beyoncé
• Create playlists, and import or export them as M3U files
• Voice notes, call recordings and short clips are left out automatically

PODCASTS AND AUDIOBOOKS
Long listens pick up where you left off, with a progress bar showing how far through you
are.

LISTENING STATS
See how much you listened today, this week, this month or of all time, with your top
songs, artists and genres.

YOURS TO KEEP
Back up your playlists, favourites, history, stats and settings to a single file, saved
wherever you like, and restore it on a new phone. Songs are matched even when the new phone
stores them differently.

MADE FOR EVERY MOMENT
• Pull down on Home for fresh Quick picks
• Sleep timer that gently fades out
• Drag to reorder your queue
• Waveform seek bar
• Autoplay similar songs when your queue ends
• Android Auto support
• Lock screen and notification controls
• Home screen widgets
• App shortcuts: long-press the icon to shuffle, resume or open Favourites
• Share a song file with friends

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Blimp has no internet access. It doesn't collect, share or upload anything. The
microphone permission is only used by Android's audio visualizer to read the music
that Blimp itself is playing. Nothing is ever recorded.

Black and yellow throughout, with a true black background that looks great on OLED
screens.

## Data safety form answers

- Does your app collect or share any of the required user data types? **No**
- Is all of the user data collected by your app encrypted in transit? Not applicable (no data collected)
- Do you provide a way for users to request that their data is deleted? Not applicable. Listening
  and search history live only on the device and can be cleared in Settings → Privacy & data.
  Backups are files the user saves themselves, where they choose; Blimp never sends them anywhere.
