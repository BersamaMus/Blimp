Blimp

Privacy Policy

Effective 9 October 2026 · Applies to the Blimp music player app for Android

At a glance

  • Blimp has no internet access. It cannot send anything anywhere.
  • We don't collect, store, sell or share any personal data.
  • Everything Blimp keeps, like playlists and listening history, stays on your phone.
  • The microphone permission is only for the visualizer. Blimp never records audio.

What Blimp does

Blimp is a music player for the audio files already on your Android device. It works entirely offline. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no third-party tracking tools in the app.

Permissions and why Blimp asks for them

Music and audio
To find and play the songs on your device, and read their details such as title, artist, album art and lyrics.
Microphone
Android requires this permission for its audio visualizer, which Blimp uses to draw bars that move with the music Blimp is playing. Blimp does not record, store or listen to sound from your microphone.
Control audio settings
For the equalizer and volume normalisation.
Keep running in the background
So music keeps playing with the screen off, with controls in the notification and on the lock screen.

Information stored on your device

To work as a music player, Blimp saves a few things on your phone. None of it ever leaves the device through Blimp:

  • Your playlists, favourites and current queue
  • Listening history, used for Home, your top songs and listening stats
  • Search history
  • Your settings, such as theme, equalizer and hidden folders

You can pause or clear your listening and search history at any time in Settings → Privacy & data. Uninstalling Blimp deletes all of it.

Device backup

If you have turned on Android's own backup, Android may include Blimp's settings and history in your device backup. That backup is made by Android and stored in your Google account under Google's privacy policy. Blimp has no access to it. You can turn Android backup off in your phone's settings.

Android Auto and sharing

If you connect to Android Auto, Blimp shows your music in your car's display through Android. When you choose to share a song, Android's share menu sends the file to the app you pick. Both happen only when you use them, and Blimp itself doesn't send data anywhere.

Children

Blimp doesn't collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted on this page with a new effective date. If Blimp ever starts to collect data, this page will say exactly what and why before that version of the app is released.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the developer email address shown on Blimp's Google Play Store listing.
