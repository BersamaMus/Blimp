Your music.
Beautifully played.

Blimp plays the music already on your PC, with a live visualizer, synced lyrics and hi-res audio. No accounts, no ads, no internet.

Download for Windows Free · Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit · All releases

Everything your music deserves

Point Blimp at your music folder and it does the rest.

Live visualizer

Bars, Mirror, Wave, Radial and Pulse, moving with every beat of what's playing.

Synced lyrics

Lines light up as they're sung. Read from your files' tags or .lrc files beside them.

Sounds right

Hi-res badges, volume normalisation, smooth fades between songs and a waveform seek bar.

Your library, your way

Songs, albums, artists, genres and folders. Albums sorted into Albums, EPs and Singles. Search that ignores accents.

Listening stats

Your top songs, artists and genres, and how much you listened today, this week or ever.

Private by design

Blimp never goes online. It doesn't collect, share or upload anything.

Made for the desktop

Not a phone app in a window. Blimp fits in with Windows.

  • Media keys and the Windows media flyout
  • Keeps playing in the tray when you close the window
  • Pauses when your headphones are unplugged
  • Sortable song table for big libraries
  • Remembers its size and place on screen
  • Shortcuts: Space play, Ctrl+←/→ skip, Ctrl+F search
Coming soon

Also on Android

The same Blimp on your phone, with Android Auto, home screen widgets, lock screen controls and a sleep timer. Coming to Google Play.

Download for Windows
Windows says it "protected your PC"?

That's because the installer isn't signed with a paid certificate yet, not because anything is wrong with it. Click More info, then Run anyway.

To uninstall, go to Settings › Apps › Installed apps, find Blimp, and choose Uninstall.
